2. What Polaris is

Polaris is an independent Minecraft launcher and configuration client. 5. Shards and cosmetics

Shards and cosmetics are local progress stored in your Polaris settings. They have no monetary value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be bought or sold. There is no checkout in this build, so there are no purchases and no refunds. We may reset or adjust local cosmetics in a future update when the feature changes.

6. Updates and installers

Polaris can check for updates and, when you choose Download Update, download and run a new installer. The app verifies the published size and SHA-256 before running it and exits before the installer touches the installation directory. Updates are provided as-is. You are responsible for keeping your own copies and for deciding whether to install a release.
